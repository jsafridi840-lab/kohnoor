Overview

Top things to do

  1. Hike up from Jandrai village through the forest
  2. Camp on the meadow and watch the sunrise
  3. Day hike on to Katora Lake
  4. Visit the waterfall on the way up

History

For generations Gujjar and Kohistani herding families have brought their cattle up to Jahaz Banda each summer; the stone huts on the meadow are their seasonal homes.

Getting there

Drive to Thall via Dir and Sheringal, take a local jeep to Jandrai, then hike or hire a horse up to the meadow.

What to pack

Where to stay & eat

Stay: Simple camps and huts run by locals on the meadow in season; bring a warm sleeping bag.

Food: Basic meals (daal, rice, tea) at the camps; carry snacks and water purification.

Good to know

  • Hire horses or porters in Jandrai if carrying heavy gear.
  • Nights are cold even in July; pack a down jacket.
