Murree

Pakistan's best-known hill station, an hour and a half from Islamabad, with Mall Road, pine ridges and winter snow.

Nathia Gali & Ayubia

The quieter, greener hill stations of the Galiyat ridge between Murree and Abbottabad: oak and pine forest, Mukshpuri and Miranjani peaks and the Pipeline walking track.
